World Name: Eclipse Veil – The Awakened Era

Core Premise and Timeline

In this world, the modern Earth map remains the foundational geography, but a cataclysmic event called "The Eclipse" on March 17, 2021, permanently altered reality. A worldwide solar eclipse coincided with an unknown cosmic phenomenon that tore "the Veil" — an invisible barrier separating mundane physics from mana-rich dimensions. Mana flooded the planet, awakening latent powers in humans, spawning dungeons and rifts, mutating wildlife into monsters, and gradually revealing or creating new races.

The current year is 2026, making this a near-contemporary setting only five years post-Eclipse. Technology still dominates daily life (smartphones, internet, electric vehicles, global supply chains), but it has fused with mana-based systems. Skyscrapers in Seoul, New York, or Shanghai now coexist with floating mage towers, enchanted bullet trains, and hunter-managed no-fly zones over high-risk rift areas. Society adapted with shocking speed due to pre-existing infrastructure, but inequalities exploded.

The System — a universal, game-like interface visible only to "Awakened" individuals — governs supernatural elements. Everyone over age 15 has a chance to Awaken, though rates vary by region and genetics (roughly 8-12% globally). Non-Awakened rely on tech, potions, and enchanted gear.

Major Historical Milestones
2021 (Year 0): Eclipse. Billions witness mana storms. First dungeons appear in remote areas (Amazon, Siberian taiga, Australian outback). Initial monster waves kill millions. Governments collapse temporarily in weaker states.
2021-2022: "Chaos Years." Major cities form Hunter Defense Zones. China and the US lead in militarized responses. First international treaties like the Mana Accord establish the Global Hunter Association (GHA).
2023-2024: Stabilization. Corporate "Awakening Pharma" and "ManaTech" conglomerates boom. New races integrate (or clash). Black markets for dungeon loot explode.
2025-2026: Current "Veil Equilibrium." Cold-war style tensions between superpowers over high-tier dungeons. Cultural renaissance mixing K-pop with cultivation sects, Hollywood blockbusters featuring real hunter stunts.
Geography and Altered Map

The physical continents and oceans are recognizable, but mana has reshaped them:

East Asia (Core Powerhouse): Korea, Japan, and coastal China are the most developed "Hunter Hubs." Seoul's Gangnam district has mana-infused skyscrapers that regenerate from damage. The Korean Peninsula has stable high-level dungeons in the DMZ (now a regulated hunting ground). Japan's islands feature "Spirit Forests" where yokai-like entities coexist with tech. China's interior has massive "Forbidden Zones" (mutated Gobi and Himalayas) controlled by state sects blending murim-style cultivation with modern military.
North America: United States dominates with megacorporations like Aether Dynamics (Silicon Valley meets hunter guilds). The Rockies and Appalachians host permanent rifts. Texas and Midwest farmlands use golem tractors and enchanted crops. Canada’s north is a monster-rich frontier. Mexico and Central America have dense jungle dungeons blending Aztec-inspired ruins with modern cartels turned hunter syndicates.
Europe: Fragmented but culturally rich. EU Hunter Command in Brussels. The Alps are "Dragon Spine" mountains with ancient awakened bloodlines. UK has "Fogveil London" where mana thickens fog into illusory mazes. Eastern Europe and Russia control vast taiga rifts, with oligarch hunters and old-world cultivation clans.
South America: Amazon has become the "Emerald Abyss" — the largest continuous dungeon-forest, home to beast tribes and unexplored high-tier zones. Brazil leads in bio-mana research. Andean nations have sky-islands formed by levitation mana.
Africa: Rapidly rising power. Sahara mana storms created "Crystal Deserts" with priceless resources. Sub-Saharan nations formed the Pan-African Awakened Union, leveraging high Awakening rates among certain populations and megafauna mutations. Egypt’s pyramids are now active mana nexuses.
Oceania and Polar Regions: Australia’s outback is a lawless hunter frontier. Antarctica has "Frostveil" research stations uncovering primordial mana sources. Pacific islands host "Atoll Realms" with merfolk and aquatic dungeons.

Rifts and Dungeons appear dynamically — temporary ones close after clearing, permanent ones are national assets. Mana density is highest near "Ley Lines" following real-world tectonic plates and ancient sacred sites.

Races and Peoples
Humans (70-75% of population): Still majority. Divided into Ordinary, Awakened (with Classes), and "Latents" (potential future Awakened). Ethnic groups retain identities but with cultural blending via global hunter culture.
Beastkin (8-10%): Emerged from mana-mutated humans or direct summoning. Cat-eared, wolf-tailed, avian-winged variants. High physical stats, popular in combat and entertainment. Strong communities in Africa, Southeast Asia, and North American reservations-turned-enclaves. Social tensions exist (prejudice vs. admiration for their agility).
Elvenkind / Fae-descended (4-5%): Graceful, long-lived (200+ years) beings tied to forests and mana springs. Many "emerged" from hidden realms or evolved from humans with high affinity. Dominant in Europe’s ancient woods and Amazon fringes. Masters of nature magic and archery. Diplomatic but isolationist tendencies.
Orc / Trollkin (3-4%): Muscular, regenerative warriors from rift spawns or mutations. Integrated into military and construction. Strong clans in Russia, Mongolia, and parts of Africa. Honor-based culture with growing urban presence.
Dwarves / Earthkin (2-3%): Stout, crafting masters who appeared near mountain ranges. Excel in enchanting tech and mining. Major guilds in Scandinavia, Andes, and Appalachians. Love heavy machinery fused with runes.
Other Minor Races: Merfolk in coastal cities, avian Harpies in high mountains, demonic-touched "Infernal" bloodlines (stigmatized but powerful), and rare dragons (mostly in human form or sealed).

Racial dynamics involve integration laws, affirmative programs for new races, and underground discrimination. Mixed heritage is increasingly common among the young.

World Dynamics: Society, Economy, Politics, and Power

The System and Classes:
Every Awakened receives a Class (e.g., Sword Saint, Archmage, Shadow Assassin, Mechanic Summoner, Cultivator, Beast Tamer). Progression via killing monsters, clearing dungeons, or training yields levels, stats (STR, AGI, MANA, etc.), skills, and titles. Top tiers reach Level 80+ (national threats/weapons). Murim-inspired "Internal Energy" cultivation coexists with Western magic systems — Korean and Chinese guilds emphasize qi meridians and pill refining.

Economy:

Mana Economy: Dungeon cores, monster crystals, and rare herbs are the new oil/gold. Global GDP tripled despite chaos.
Hunter Corporations: Guilds like Raijin Group (Korea/Japan), Eternal Dragon Clan (China), Liberty Vanguard (US) act as mega-corps with private armies, streaming platforms (real dungeon raids on Twitch-like services), and R&D.
Black Market: Illegal rift poaching, forbidden artifacts, and "Awakening serums" fuel crime.
Daily Life: Apps show real-time rift alerts. Schools teach basic mana control. Hospitals use healing magic alongside surgery. Influencers are S-rank hunters with millions of followers.

Politics:

Superpowers: US-China cold war over dungeon resources, with Europe, India, and the African Union as swing powers. UN replaced by GHA Council.
Conflicts: Proxy wars in rift-rich regions (Middle East now has "Djinn Rifts"). Terrorist groups like "Veil Purists" (anti-mana extremists) and "Abyssal Cults" (monster worshippers).
Laws: Awakened must register. High-rankers face conscription in crises. Ethical debates on monster sentience and race rights.

Culture and Daily Life:
Pop culture glorifies hunters — manhwa-style comics are documentaries, idols train in dual cultivation/idol schools. Fashion mixes streetwear with enchanted armor. Religions adapted: many see the Eclipse as divine, others as scientific multiversal event. Sports include "Mana Olympics" with powered events.

Threats and Frontiers:

Deep Dungeons: Mythical floors with god-like bosses rumored to grant immortality or world-altering wishes.
Dimensional Overlords: Ancient entities stirring beyond the Veil.
Inequality: Top 1% hunters control vast wealth; ordinary people in safe zones live comfortably, but frontier regions suffer raids.
Hopeful Elements: International hunter teams, breakthroughs in mana-tech curing diseases, space programs using teleport gates.

This world feels alive and modern: you can order coffee via app in a Seoul cafe while watching a live raid on your phone, then take a bullet train to a dungeon gate for a weekend hunt. Ancient murim sects run dojos in skyscrapers, elven diplomats negotiate at the UN, and a young Korean-American hunter might discover their latent Dragonblood lineage while grinding low-tier rifts in California.

The balance between wonder, danger, opportunity, and human (plus non-human) drama makes it ripe for stories of personal growth, faction wars, or exploration. If you'd like expansions on specific regions, character templates, magic rules, or even a starter plot hook, just say the word!
