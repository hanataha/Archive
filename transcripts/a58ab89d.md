# 55. Eon Reborn as First Son of a Japanese Conglomerate (Archive adaptation)

Source: Grok chat a58ab89d ("New Eon Story as Baby in Japanese Conglomerate"), 120 turns. Rewritten adaptation, approved by the owner on 2026-10-31. Only the adapted text below is canon for the Archive; the raw transcript is not published.

## Part I: The Kurogane heir (non-sexual; all characters here are family, staff, or school friends)

**Birth.** Eon, carrying the memories of his earlier lives, is born in a private suite of the Kurogane Medical Tower in Minato, Tokyo. He is the first son of Kurogane Ren (41), head of the Kurogane Group, and Kurogane Ayame (née Fujiwara, 32). The Kurogane Group is a 140-year-old conglomerate founded in the Meiji era, with interests in specialty steel, energy, real estate, finance, dual-use technology and luxury retail. The family lives on a walled estate in Meguro. His aunt Kurogane Shiori (35) runs a real-estate arm and lives in her own wing; cousin Takeshi is an executive in the materials division. Eon keeps his old mind hidden and grows up in the ordinary way.

**Childhood friends.** At five he plays with daughters of allied families, among them Aoi, Hana, Rin, and later Sora and Yui, and quietly teaches them things that impress them: balancing block towers, reading maps, fixing sums.

**School years.** Through elementary school at a private academy, then public junior high and public senior high in Tokyo, the friends stay a loyal circle around him. They ask for his help and look to him as their fixed point. He stays low-key, neither first nor last in class. They graduate senior high together, then go on to university, and the circle stays close as friends. (Every school-age scene is entirely non-sexual.)

## Part II: Mei (adults only; begins after Eon graduates from university)

**Assignment.** After Eon finishes university (age ~22), the Kisaragi family, Kurogane retainers for four generations, assigns their daughter **Kisaragi Mei (24–25)** as his personal attendant and chief aide. She has had no earlier contact with him. She is capable and composed, and soon runs his household and schedule better than anyone.

**Her choice.** Working beside him, Mei comes to respect and then love him. One night she tells him plainly that she chooses to serve him alone for life, as her own decision, and asks whether he wants her. He makes sure she means it; she says yes clearly and asks him to seal the pledge. They become lovers. Their relationship is intensely physical and openly kinky (devotion, collar-and-leash play, using her body as his "furniture", extreme degradation games she asks for), always at her request or with her explicit, enthusiastic agreement. She keeps a safeword and uses check-ins.

**The collar.** He gives her a collar and leash as a gift; she renews her pledge, happy and lucid. He tells her he wants all of her, the whole woman and not only her body, and she becomes more lively and herself because of it.

**Revelation and power.** Months later he reveals his true nature and powers. At her request he remakes her body to her liking (more beautiful and larger-breasted, sustained by him) and grants her powers: self-cleaning, instant change of clothing, creation for his sake, and eventually outer-goddess-level control of time, space, creation and destruction, which she must still grow into. Her chosen signature power is to split into as many bodies as she likes while remaining one mind. The bodies share and multiply sensation, and in a private domain she forms everything around him from herself: chairs, beds, baths, even a horse. When going out among people she keeps a single, dressed form.

**Ending point.** The chat ends with Eon and Mei alone in a world of her own making, where everything he touches except his food is her.
