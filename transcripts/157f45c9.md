Phase 3: The Heart-to-Heart

Eon’s throat tightened. Aiko glanced between them, something unreadable flickering in her hazel eyes.

Fleurdelys turned to her fully, violet gaze steady. “Aiko-chan. You care for him too. I see it—the way you light up when he enters a room, the way you save him the last melon soda. Aiko blinked rapidly, cheeks flushing. “I—wow. That’s… I mean, we’re not official or anything, but…” She looked at Eon, vulnerable. “Do you want this? Both of us?”

Eon opened his mouth. Closed it. Opened it again. “I… don’t want to hurt anyone. Aiko, you make work feel like a con. Fleur, you’re… my dream made real. I don’t know how to choose.”

Fleurdelys squeezed his hand. “Then do not choose. Yet. Let us write the next chapter together. All three of us.”

Aiko bit her lip, then—surprising everyone—laughed. A bright, genuine sound. “This is the weirdest love triangle ever. But… okay. Trial period. Like a beta test. We date, we game, we see where the story goes. No save-scumming.”

Fleurdelys’s smile could have powered Tokyo. “A beta. I accept.”
